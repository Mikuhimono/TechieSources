TechieSources Purpose

The primary purpose of this website is to serve as a centralized platform that compiles and organizes Review of Related Literature (RRL) specifically within the field of Information and Communications Technology (ICT). By providing a well-structured and accessible repository of ICT-related references, the website aims to support the academic and research needs of ICT students in Cainta Senior High School. It is designed to help students locate relevant literature more efficiently, thereby reducing the time and effort traditionally required in gathering research materials. Moreover, this platform seeks to foster a culture of academic excellence and innovation by ensuring that students have convenient access to credible and updated scholarly resources. Ultimately, the website functions as both a learning aid and a research tool that contributes to the development of students’ knowledge, critical thinking, and research capabilities in the ICT domain.
